Privacy Policy and Personal Data Notice (KVKK)

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Version 5.0 (first App Store release) · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This notice is prepared under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). It is not a contract; it only informs you, and we do not ask for your consent to it. If the Turkish and English texts differ, the Turkish text prevails.

In short

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, e-mail, password or phone number.
  • Your island, tasks, ideas, wishes and study records are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to any other server.
  • In this version, the app does not send your personal data to any server, in Türkiye or abroad. Friends, announcements, special quests and gift codes are not part of this version.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. We never see your card details, your name or your Apple account details.
  • We don't sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across other apps or websites.

1. Data controller

The Başar! app and the basarapp.com website are offered by Tunç Kurt, an independent developer living in Türkiye (a private individual, not a company). Under KVKK, Tunç Kurt is the data controller. "We" in this notice means him.

  • E-mail: support@basarapp.com

2. What data we process, and how

a) Data that stays only on your device

The following is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage and in the last 14 days of backups. It is not sent to us. If your device's own backup (e.g. iCloud backup) is on, it is included there and Apple keeps it under its agreement with you.

  • Focus sessions: length, start and end time, result, and the folder and subject names you pick.
  • Tasks, ideas and wishes: the text you write and whether they are completed.
  • Study tools (optional): numbers of questions solved, weekly goals, exam dates.
  • End-of-session mood note (optional).
  • Island and game progress: plants, buildings, Knowledge Points (BP), quests, achievements, settings.
  • Optional profile: your card name (the name you'd like on your share card), your character and your birthday (day and month only), used to show a cake on your island that day.
  • Trial and membership: when your trial started, the membership you chose and its end date. This is read from the App Store on your device.
  • Consent records: the date, text version, language and chosen product when you accepted the Terms of Use and the purchase information, kept as proof.
  • Backup files: a file you create with Settings → Export backup is saved only where you choose. A backup you put in the Başar! folder on your device (On My iPhone or On My iPad) in the Files app is read by the app only when you tap Settings → "Load from Başar! folder".

b) Trial and purchases (Apple)

  • The free trial, memberships and the lifetime purchase are sold only through Apple's App Store. Apple takes the payment.
  • Your card details, name, e-mail and Apple account details never reach us.
  • To unlock what you bought, the app asks the App Store, from within your device, which Başar! products your Apple account owns. The answer is used only on your device.
  • Apple gives us sales reports by country, product and amount that don't identify you.
  • Apple processes purchase and subscription data under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

c) Share card

  • The "Share your results" card is created entirely on your device. It shows a picture of your island, your total focus time for the day or week, your sessions, streak and plant count and (if you set one) your card name. Your tasks, ideas, wishes and subjects are never on the card.
  • The card is shared only when you tap "Share" and pick a destination from your device's share menu. We never receive it.
  • The app or person you share it with (e.g. Instagram, WhatsApp) handles it under their own rules.

d) Notifications

Session reminders are created on your device. No information is sent to any server for them.

e) When you write to us

If you write to support@basarapp.com, we use your e-mail address and message only to reply and, if a dispute arises, as proof.

f) basarapp.com

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or tracking; your language choice is stored only in your browser. GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses and technical logs to serve the site.

3. Data we don't use

We don't use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or advertising identifier. The app has no ads, ad networks, analytics or tracking tools. If we ever add one, we will update this notice beforehand and tell you in the app.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

Most data in the app is processed only on your device and never reaches us. For data that reaches us or is processed on our behalf:

5. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

  • Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland and Apple Inc., USA): distribution, trial, memberships and purchases. Apple processes this data under its own responsibility; we don't send Apple any information about you.
  • Google (Gmail) and Porkbun: receiving and forwarding support e-mails (USA).
  • GitHub (Microsoft): hosting basarapp.com (USA).
  • Competent public authorities: only when required by law.

Transfers abroad

This version of the app does not send your personal data abroad. Data is processed abroad only when:

  • you e-mail us: your e-mail is processed on servers in the USA (Google, Porkbun); this transfer is occasional and necessary to answer your request (KVKK Art. 9/6-b);
  • you visit basarapp.com: your IP address and technical logs are processed on GitHub's servers in the USA.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you delete the app or use Settings → Reset everything (and in your device's own backup for as long as that backup exists).
  • Consent records: on your device until the app is deleted; also inside any backup file you export.
  • Support correspondence: 10 years, as proof in possible disputes (general limitation period, Turkish Code of Obligations Art. 146).
  • Sales reports: for the period required by tax law.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, know who it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, ask for correction if incomplete or wrong, ask for deletion or destruction, ask that these be notified to recipients, object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

  • Deletion: data on your device is deleted when you delete the app or use Settings → Reset everything. We hold no copy of it.
  • Getting a copy: Settings → Export backup.
  • Other requests: e-mail support@basarapp.com. We answer within 30 days at the latest, as a rule free of charge (Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller).
  • If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK).

8. Children

Başar! is designed for students of all ages. No account is needed and no contact details are asked from children. If you are under 18, use the app with your parent's or guardian's knowledge and make purchases with their consent. Parents can turn on Apple's "Ask to Buy" on their child's device and can reach us at support@basarapp.com.

9. Security

Your data is kept on your device, in the app's own storage that other apps can't access. We have no server holding your data. We recommend keeping your device's screen lock and backup turned on.

10. Changes

We may update this notice. We announce important changes in the app before they take effect. If we later add an online section such as Friends, we will update this notice and ask for any necessary consents before it is switched on. The current version is always at https://basarapp.com/en/privacy.html.
